# How CamPOS records the way you use it

*Draft wording for the pilot agreement and the consent form. Written to be read by
the pharmacy, in plain English. Camdianz reviews and a lawyer checks before signing.*

---

## In one sentence

CamPOS keeps a short note each time a screen is opened or a job is finished, so we
can see which parts of the software pharmacies actually use — and nothing else.

---

## What is recorded

Each note has **six pieces of information** and no more:

| | |
|---|---|
| **When** | the date and time |
| **What** | which screen was opened, or which job was done — from a fixed list, printed below |
| **How it ended** | finished, did not finish, opened, or skipped |
| **Which kind of account** | administrator or staff — **not which person** |
| **Which version** | the version of CamPOS installed |
| **A reference number** | so the same note is not counted twice |

### The fixed list

These are the only things CamPOS writes down. Nothing can be added to this list
without changing this document first.

**Screens opened** — sales, stock-in, inventory, alerts, reports, sales history,
stock history, products, data centre

**Jobs finished** — a sale, a stock-in, a stock adjustment, a refund, a backup, a
manual send to the server, a receipt printed, a label printed, a data import

**Alerts** — opening the expiry list, opening the low-stock list

**Basic** — the app starting, someone logging in

---

## What is **not** recorded

This is the more important list.

- **No patient or customer information.** None is held anywhere in CamPOS.
- **No medicine names.** A note says "a sale was finished", never what was sold.
- **No money.** No prices, no totals, no takings.
- **No text you type.** Not searches, not notes, not batch numbers.
- **No pictures of your screen**, and no record of what you type on the keyboard.
- **No record of which member of staff did what.** CamPOS writes down whether the
  account was an administrator or a staff account, and stops there. There is no way
  for us to tell who was at the counter.

---

## Who can see it

Only Camdianz. The notes are sent to the Camdianz server together with the rest of
your CamPOS data, over an encrypted connection, and are read by the small number of
people at Camdianz who work on the software.

They are not sold, not shared with other pharmacies, and not given to anyone else.

> **Separate from this:** the antibiotic report you submit for antimicrobial
> resistance research is a different thing entirely. It leaves the pharmacy for a
> research body, it carries no money and no pharmacy name, and it has nothing to do
> with these usage notes.

---

## Why we keep it

So we can answer three questions honestly:

1. **Which parts of CamPOS help, and which parts nobody uses.** We would rather
   remove a feature than leave it in the way.
2. **Where it breaks.** If printing fails often at one pharmacy, we would like to
   know before you have to tell us.
3. **Whether it is being used at all.** If a pharmacy stops using CamPOS, we want to
   find out why, not wait and wonder.

We also report to funders on how the pilot went. Those reports use totals across all
pharmacies — never one pharmacy on its own.

---

## Your choices

- You may **ask to see** everything recorded about your pharmacy. We will send it.
- You may **ask us to delete** it. We will, and you can keep using CamPOS.
- If you decide to stop taking part in the pilot, we will delete it then as well.

CamPOS keeps working the same either way. Nothing here changes what the software
does for you.

---

## Questions

Ask Camdianz at any time. If anything above turns out not to match what CamPOS
actually does, tell us — that is a fault on our side, and we will fix the software
rather than the wording.
